01 · Method

From one image to a simulation-ready digital twin

CoVeTwin predicts compact part geometry and physical structure with a VLM, verifies multiple voxel candidates, restores detailed surfaces with an image-conditioned flow decoder, transfers part labels, and exports articulated URDF and MuJoCo XML assets.

04 · Benchmark articulation

Generated assets under physics-engine joint actuation

Each video shows the textured, part-decomposed CoVeTwin URDF under PyBullet joint playback. Use the controls to pause, scrub, or replay.

These clips are qualitative articulation demonstrations. The paper's execution-rate table is measured separately with MuJoCo loading and finite-state simulation checks.

05 · Real-world generalization

Articulated reconstruction from ordinary images

Selected examples demonstrate that CoVeTwin can infer coherent movable structure and detailed appearance from images outside the curated benchmark rendering setup.

Real-world cross-method comparison. Each row shows the photographed input followed by five methods under the same articulation controls.

06 · Cross-method dynamics

Common-input articulation comparison across methods

Seven benchmark inputs are replayed under the same viewer and physics controls. Each row compares Articulate Anything, PhysX-3D, PhysX-Anything, URDF-Anything, and CoVeTwin. The broad comparison makes missing outputs, fragmented geometry, incomplete parts, and unstable articulation directly inspectable rather than relying on selected still frames alone.

Each card points to the method output for the same input. All videos are H.264 MP4 playbacks under PyBullet joint control.

07 · Dynamic ablation

Representation and verification effects over complete motion

Every benchmark case is shown with direct voxel coordinates, absolute voxel indices, absolute spans, relative occupancy spans without verification, and the complete CoVeTwin model. The first four variants isolate the geometry target, while the last pair isolates connectivity-based candidate verification.

These videos are qualitative PyBullet playbacks. They complement, but do not replace, the separately reported MuJoCo execution-rate test.
